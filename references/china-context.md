# Understanding China / 了解中国

The English explanations are project-authored explanatory translations, not official translations.

| 中文 | Explanatory English | Distinction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 五年规划纲要 | Five-Year Plan outline | National development framework; a business still needs implementing rules and market evidence. |
| 规划建议 | Recommendations for formulating a plan | Earlier planning document; not identical to the final outline. |
| 预期性指标 | Anticipated / indicative target | Guidance is not an individual entitlement or guaranteed outcome. Verify each indicator's classification. |
| 约束性指标 | Binding target in the planning implementation framework | Responsibility for implementation does not alone establish a specific legal duty on a foreign firm. |
| 专项/区域规划 | Sectoral / regional plan | Follow how national direction is translated into sectors and locations. |
| 年度计划与预算 | Annual plan and budget | Connect five-year ambition to annual execution. |
| 投资于人 | Investment in people | Human capabilities and public services; not a promise of personal subsidies. |
| 新质生产力 | New quality productive forces | Explain productivity, innovation and upgrading mechanisms, not just fashionable technologies. |
| 全国统一大市场 | A unified national market | Rules, competition and connectivity; not proof all local barriers have disappeared. |
| 反内卷 | Addressing destructive / involution-style competition | Do not infer guaranteed higher prices or margins. |
| 制度型开放 | Institutional opening-up | Rules, regulations, management and standards; check implementing instruments. |
| 负面清单 | Negative list | General access, foreign investment and cross-border services are distinct regimes. |
| 试点 | Pilot programme | Location, entity, activity and conditions matter. |
| 准入又准营 | Ability to operate as well as enter | Separate establishment/access from operating permissions. |
| 产业链自主可控 | Greater autonomous capability in industrial chains | Supplier opportunities and substitution pressure can coexist. |
| 银发经济 | Silver economy | Separate user, purchaser and payer. |
| 碳排放双控 | Dual control of total carbon emissions and intensity | Planning direction does not establish a current product certification requirement. |
| 因地制宜 | Adaptation to local conditions | Every locality and person need not pursue the same industry. |

Explain the term, cite the Chinese passage, describe the mechanism and audience implications, and state what the plan cannot establish. For “what is happening now?”, retrieve dated implementation evidence; the bundled corpus supplies background.
