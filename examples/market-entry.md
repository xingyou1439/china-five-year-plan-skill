# China market-entry example

This is a hypothetical demonstration, not a verified regulatory opinion or investment case.

**Prompt:** We are an overseas eldercare technology company. Does the 15th Five-Year Plan mean we can operate eldercare facilities anywhere in China?

**Illustrative response:** Ageing and eldercare are directly relevant policy themes, but nationwide operating permission is unverified. Chapter 40, page 56 of the supplied outline (SRC-DEB47DA253), discusses community eldercare and care capacity. Chapter 21, page 35, describes orderly services opening and pilots. Those planning statements do not establish your company's eligibility for a specific operation in every region.

Separate supplying equipment, licensing software, distribution, partnerships and operating facilities. The appropriate choice depends on your product, customer, proposed entity, target city and whether the service includes regulated care or handles personal/health data. Do not infer jurisdiction from the language used in the prompt.

**Policy fact:** eldercare capacity and services opening appear in the outline. **Inference:** local partnerships and a clearly defined product/service role may be worth researching. **Missing evidence:** customers and payers, current applicable access/operating rules, local standards and data requirements.

Before a definitive entry recommendation, retrieve the current primary rules for that activity, location and entity. If browsing or those rules are unavailable, mark entry eligibility unverified. A useful initial experiment can be customer/partner interviews and a defined demonstration whose permitted scope has been verified. Do not advise commencing an unverified regulated operation.
