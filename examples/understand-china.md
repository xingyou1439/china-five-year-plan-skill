# Understanding China example

**Prompt:** What does "investment in people" mean in China's 15th Five-Year Plan? Is it a new cash subsidy for individuals?

**Illustrative response:** In this outline, the term connects human capabilities and public-service investment. [Chapter 16 of the 15th Five-Year Plan](https://www.bjrd.gov.cn/zyfb/zt/14jqgrd04chybjdbthdztbd/wjbg/202603/t20260314_4557219.html) discusses human-resource development and projects in elder/child services, primary health care, education and vocational skills. This is a planning direction; it does not establish that every individual is entitled to a cash payment.

To understand implementation, follow the relevant sectoral/regional plan, annual programme, budget and eligibility rules. Chapter 62, describes how planning tasks connect to annual plans and implementation arrangements. Any particular subsidy requires its own implementing document.

The English wording is an explanatory translation. This is a knowledge question, so the Skill should explain directly without requiring a business budget or recommending a personal venture.
